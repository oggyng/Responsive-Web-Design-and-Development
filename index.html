How It Works

Five easy stops to your next ride.

  1. STEP 01 / 05

    Join CPU

    Sign up with your student ID. For drivers, you'll also need to add your car details and wait for admin approval.

  2. STEP 02 / 05

    Browse trips

    Choose the week, day, direction, pickup station and time, then hit Search.

  3. STEP 03 / 05

    Choose a ride

    See each driver's car, seats left, fee and CO₂ saved. Sort by Recommended, Lowest fee or Highest rating.

  4. STEP 04 / 05

    Request & ride

    Requests open every Saturday and Sunday for next week. See the fee first, and get a notification once your driver accepts.

  5. STEP 05 / 05

    Save CO₂, level up

    Rate each other after the ride. Every trip adds to your CO₂ saved, earns badges and moves you up the leaderboard.
